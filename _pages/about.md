Rumana Yasmin is a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP), Faculty of Science & Technology (FST), where she has been serving since 2024. She completed both her B.Sc. and M.Sc. (Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering from Jahangirnagar University.
Her research interests lie in machine learning, computer vision, graph neural networks (GNN), natural language processing (NLP), and explainable AI (XAI), with a particular focus on real-world applications in healthcare and smart systems. She is passionate about research that bridges theory and practice to create impactful solutions.She is a recipient of the National Science and Technology (NST) Fellowship (2022–23) and multiple government merit scholarships.
