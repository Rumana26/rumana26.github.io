My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning**, **computer vision**, and **intelligent systems**, with a strong focus on applications that have real-world societal impact — particularly in **healthcare** and **smart systems**.

---

- **Intelligent Systems for Healthcare** 

---

## Current Research Directions

### Low-Resource and Indigenous Language Processing
Working on speech recognition and script classification for the Chakma language — an endangered indigenous language of Bangladesh — using deep learning and evolutionary algorithms.

### Bangla Language Technology
Contributing to datasets and models for Bangla regional dialect translation (ONUBAD dataset) and Bangla sign language recognition.

### Computer Vision for Biodiversity and Agriculture
Applying deep learning for butterfly species recognition and plant disease detection to support sustainable agriculture and biodiversity studies.
